Salaty For kids

Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Short version

Salaty collects no data about you. Everything the app needs, including your location, stays on your phone. The Android app doesn't even have permission to use the internet, so it can't send anything anywhere.

What the app stores, and where

What Why Where it stays How to delete it

Your settings (calculation method, alerts, sound choices, adjustments)To remember your choices (including athan voice, reminder and iqama sounds)Only on your phoneSettings → Reset, or uninstall the app

The app does not back up this data to Google Drive, iCloud, or any server.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting services, no tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is no data to share: nothing leaves your phone.
  • No precise location: the app asks only for your approximate location, and only when you tap "Use my location" or "Use compass".
  • No background location: the location is read only while the app is open.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): to calculate prayer times for where you are, and for the compass on the Qibla screen. You can pick a city instead.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the athan and reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, Android): so the athan arrives on time, not minutes late.
  • Start at boot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, Android): to set your alerts again after the phone restarts.
  • Do Not Disturb access (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY, Android, optional): only if you turn on auto-silent, to silence the phone during prayer and restore it afterwards.
  • Motion sensor and a short background task (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, WAKE_LOCK, Android, optional): only if you turn on flip-to-mute, and only while an athan is ringing, to notice the phone being turned face-down. Nothing is recorded.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): for the vibrate option and haptic feedback on the Qibla screen.
  • Sound settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): to play athan and sound previews inside the app.
  • App icon badges (added by the notifications library, e.g. for Samsung, Huawei or Oppo home screens): Salaty never shows a badge on its icon; these do nothing.

When Salaty opens another app

  • "Mosques near me": opens your maps app with a search for mosques around your saved location. Salaty hands that location to the maps app, whose own privacy rules then apply. Salaty itself still sends nothing over the internet.
  • Web links: the app may open this privacy page or its store page in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy rules; the app itself sends nothing.

Battery optimisation

The app checks whether Android battery optimisation is enabled for Salaty to show a tip if alerts might be delayed. Nothing is stored or transmitted.

Children

Salaty is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. It has no ads, no chat and no purchases.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. We will never start collecting data without saying so here first, and in the app.

Salaty and your privacy (for kids)

  • Salaty shows prayer times and plays the athan.
  • It does not collect anything about you. Not your name, not where you are, nothing.
  • It uses your phone's location only to work out the prayer times. That information stays on your phone.
  • "Mosques near me" opens your phone's maps app; ask a parent before using it.
  • There are no ads and no chat.
  • If you have questions, ask a parent to email us at innovaxons.apps@gmail.com.
